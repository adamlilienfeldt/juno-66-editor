Parameters

Scroll over a bar to nudge it, hold Shift for steps of 8. The coloured dot is where each controller number came from — hover it, and hover a group heading for its notes. Numbers came from the chart on page 23 of the manual, which is a figure rather than text and was transcribed by hand; correct any of them in place and it will be remembered.

Tuning

Sends a MIDI Tuning Standard bulk dump into one of the four custom scale slots. The juno-66 keeps these over a power cycle.

Discovery

For finding the real CC numbers by ear. Hold a note, sweep a controller, listen for what moves.

Received

The juno-66 sends on two channels at once — "as played" (the keyboard) and "as sounds" (what the arp, chord and mono modes actually produce). It also sends a program change when the play mode changes.
